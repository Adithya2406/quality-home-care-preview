Quality Home Care Providers and NousLogic Announce Collaboration to Advance Connected Home Care

LEONIA, N.J. — September 2026 — Quality Home Care Providers (QHCP), a New Jersey home care organization serving patients and families since 1991, is pleased to announce a collaboration with NousLogic, a connected-health technology company, focused on expanding access to technology-enabled care in the home.

The collaboration brings together QHCP’s more than three decades of experience supporting patients in their homes with NousLogic’s connected-device and remote monitoring technology infrastructure.

Together, the organizations will explore opportunities to support healthcare providers and their patients through connected health solutions designed to make it easier for health information collected in the home to be securely shared with participating care teams.

QHCP will bring its experience in home-based services, patient engagement, education, logistical support, and healthcare-provider coordination. NousLogic will provide the technology infrastructure supporting compatible connected devices, secure data transmission, and remote monitoring capabilities.

The collaboration reflects a shared belief that the home will play an increasingly important role in the future of healthcare.

“For more than three decades, Quality Home Care Providers has focused on helping New Jersey patients receive the support they need in the place they know best — their home,” said Abraham Kazmir of Quality Home Care Providers. “Working with NousLogic gives us an opportunity to combine that experience with connected technology and explore new ways to support patients, families, and healthcare providers.”

The organizations intend to focus initially on opportunities in New Jersey, including populations that may face barriers to accessing routine healthcare services because of age, mobility, transportation, geography, or other practical challenges.

By combining established home-care capabilities with modern connected-health infrastructure, QHCP and NousLogic hope to contribute to a healthcare environment that is increasingly connected, accessible, and centered around the patient.

About Quality Home Care Providers

Founded in 1991, Quality Home Care Providers is a New Jersey-based home care organization serving patients and families throughout the state. QHCP provides home-based services and support with a longstanding commitment to quality, accessibility, and patient-centered care.

About NousLogic

NousLogic develops connected-health technology and infrastructure designed to support remote monitoring and other digital health applications. Its technology enables compatible connected devices to securely transmit information for use by participating healthcare organizations and care teams.
