About us

Serving Patients Since 1991.

For more than three decades, Quality Home Care Providers has been committed to helping patients receive the medical equipment and support they need to live safely and comfortably in their own homes.

Headquartered in Leonia, New Jersey, our company provides dependable home medical equipment and durable medical supplies to patients, families, caregivers, and healthcare providers. Our team works closely with physicians and medical professionals to ensure patients receive the equipment they need quickly, safely, and with the guidance necessary to use it properly.

We provide a wide range of home medical equipment including oxygen concentrators, portable oxygen tanks, CPAP and BiPAP machines, nebulizers, walkers, wheelchairs, shower chairs, suction machines, and other essential medical supplies that help patients manage their health from the comfort of home.

Quality Home Care Providers operates as a licensed New Jersey Health Care Service Firm, meeting strict state regulatory standards designed to protect patients and ensure reliable medical equipment services. For decades, physicians, hospitals, caregivers, and families have trusted our team to deliver the equipment and support patients depend on during some of the most important moments of their lives.

Our work is about more than equipment. Every oxygen machine delivered, every walker fitted, and every piece of equipment installed represents a patient who wants to remain safe, independent, and comfortable in their own home.

Many of the individuals we serve are elderly, recovering from illness or surgery, managing chronic conditions, or caring for loved ones who require medical support at home. Our team understands that access to reliable medical equipment can make an enormous difference in a person's quality of life.

As a small and dedicated team with more than 25 years of experience, we take pride in providing responsive service, dependable equipment, and compassionate support to the patients and families who rely on us.

Today, Quality Home Care Providers proudly serves patients across New Jersey and supports healthcare providers and caregivers who depend on reliable home medical equipment for the people they care about most.

Our mission is simple, to help patients receive the care they need in the place they feel most comfortable, their home.

As a family owned business, we believe the relationships we build with our patients matter just as much as the equipment we deliver. Many of the individuals we serve have been with us for years, and our drivers and patient care team often develop long standing relationships with the patients and families who rely on us. When our team enters a patient’s home to deliver equipment, set up a machine, or provide support, they are not simply performing a service. They are helping someone maintain their comfort, independence, and quality of life.

Over time, these relationships become something deeper than a transaction. Our drivers, technicians, and staff come to know the people they serve, and our patients come to know the people who care for them. Whether we have the privilege of welcoming patients into our office, visiting them in their homes, or shipping equipment to families across the country, we approach every interaction with the same belief: the people we serve are not just customers. They are part of the extended family that has supported our company for more than 25 years.
